Universal vs Hegemonic International Law Quiz

This quiz is designed to show how much do you support Universal or Hegemonic International Law frameworks in the International Criminal Court.

The Universal International Law framework allows the ICC to apply the same international law everywhere, and sanction countries objectively rather than based on geopolitical inclination. The Hegemonic International Law is the current framework (as of 2024) that only sanctions western rivals and neutrals based on international law and immunize powerful allies.
